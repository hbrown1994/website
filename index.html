About

English | Deutsch

Hunter Brown is a Berlin-based composer, improviser, and sound artist. His work treats the infrastructure of digital audio — codecs, machine listening, analysis, [re]synthesis — as material for reorienting the act of listening. His work takes the form of fixed-media composition, music for instruments and electronics, multichannel sound installation, and custom computer music systems. Boring Like A Drill has described his work as taking “a David Tudor-like approach to glitch, interrogating the principle upon which the digital error operates and then applying it as a compositional method.”

Improvisation is central to Hunter’s performance practice. He regularly performs with his Improvisation Instrument, a custom system developed in SuperCollider, both as a soloist and in collaborative projects including Technical Reserve and Other Plastics: “a set full of crackling energy, tensile yet static, the audience hanging on every sound” (The Wire).

His work has been presented at Ars Electronica (Linz), Darmstädter Ferienkurse (Darmstadt), Big Ears (Knoxville), Cafe OTO (London), IRCAM Manifeste (Paris), Festival Nueva Ópera (Buenos Aires), Lobe 4DSOUND (Vancouver), and KM28 (Berlin), and he has presented research at the International Computer Music Conference, the Conference on AI Music Creativity, and NIME. He has been commissioned by Ensemble Dal Niente, Line Upon Line Percussion, and Fundación Williams, and his work has been performed by the Mivos Quartet, Fonema Consort, and the Nois Saxophone Quartet. Derivatives was a finalist in the 2025 Prix CIME Electroacoustic Music Competition.

Hunter holds a BM from the Oberlin Conservatory and an MA in Digital Musics from Dartmouth College, and is a PhD candidate in composition at the University of Chicago. He works as a freelance audio engineer, serves as technical director for Ensemble Dal Niente, and co-founded the record label Party Perfect. He has taught electronic music at the Oberlin Conservatory of Music and the University of Chicago, and is currently a freelance tutor at the Catalyst Institute for Creative Arts and Technology in Berlin.

Hunter also works as a recording, mixing, and mastering engineer for new music, classical, jazz, and experimental music. He is active as an interpreter and performer of live electronic and electroacoustic music, having performed with ensembles such as Ensemble Dal Niente and EnsembleKollektiv, and designs custom software systems for live electronics and sound installation. He is available for private lessons and workshops in music production, composition, and computer music programming. See Services →
